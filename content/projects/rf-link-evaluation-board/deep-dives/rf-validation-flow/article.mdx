## Test Setup

Describe instruments, cables, calibration state, fixtures, power levels, and termination conditions.

## Validation Sequence

1. Inspect launches and RF path continuity.
2. Measure insertion loss and return loss.
3. Validate forward and reflected detector outputs.
4. Sweep mismatch conditions.
5. Log telemetry and compare against instruments.
